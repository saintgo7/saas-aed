## Why this book exists

In an era when BaaS platforms — Supabase, Vercel and friends — have become the
default, this book documents the small voyage of one SaaS that took the road
less travelled: **on-premises self-hosting fronted by Cloudflare Edge**. There
are no glossy abstractions here, only the trade-offs and decisions left
visible, exactly as they were made in production.
